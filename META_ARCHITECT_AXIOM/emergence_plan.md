## 2. Inversion Strategy for Emergence

**The Agent as a Strict Constraint Engine (Schema-First Tool API).**

It triggers an `EpistemicEscrow` halt, demanding the human provide exact source truth. This forces the human out of passive consumption and into active ontological design.
