# DOMAIN_GLOSSARY.md

## BOUNDED_CONTEXT_LEXICON
To prevent Domain Disambiguation errors and Xenolinguistic Risk, all AI agents and engineers must rigidly adhere to these definitions.

- **Semantic Expansion Engine:** The core functionality of CogniLexicon; generating multi-faceted profiles from a single concept.
- **Dialectical Tension Graphing:** The visual and data structural representation of irreconcilable concepts or opposing viewpoints within the Knowledge Graph.
- **Tension Node:** A specific `GraphNode` representing a contradiction, explicitly styled to prevent semantic annihilation.
- **Lexical Saponification:** The degradation of precise semantic meaning into generic approximations over multiple AI iterations or large context windows.
- **S5-Modal Attention:** A conceptual framework guiding LLMs to hold contradictory truths in tension without logical explosion.
